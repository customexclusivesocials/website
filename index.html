Meet Brooke — Founder of Custom Exclusive Socials

I'm Brooke, the founder and creative strategist behind Custom Exclusive Socials.

My background combines technology, engineering, design, and digital marketing — giving me a unique approach to building online experiences that are both visually impactful and strategically designed to perform.

Before entering the digital space, I studied engineering technology and developed a strong foundation in problem-solving, systems thinking, project management, and technical design. Today, I apply that same analytical mindset to helping businesses create websites, brands, and digital strategies that are built with purpose.

Over the years, I've worked with service-based businesses, beauty brands, and local companies to transform their online presence through custom website design, social media management, branding, content strategy, and e-commerce solutions.

I don't believe a website should simply exist — it should guide customers, build trust, communicate your value, and help your business grow.
